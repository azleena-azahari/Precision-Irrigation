About the calculations and the DRL policy

FAO-56. ET0 comes from the Penman-Monteith equation, using the day's temperature and humidity (daily maximum and minimum are shifted when you edit the mean) plus the additional data. Kc is the stage value (0.35, 0.75, 1.15, 0.95). In mid-season and late-season it is adjusted for wind, minimum humidity and crop height. ETc = Kc × ET0.

DRL. A Deep Q-Network chooses 0, 5, 10, 15 or 20 mm each day from the root-zone depletion, growth stage, ETc, rain today and tomorrow, and the remaining 150 mm irrigation budget. It was trained in the same water-balance simulator as the Colab notebook. The rain "forecast" for today is the rainfall value above, and tomorrow's rain is taken from the dataset.

Soil assumptions (not measured in the dataset): field capacity per plot is the 98th percentile of its soil moisture, wilting point is half of that, root depth grows from 0.2 m to 1.0 m by 70 days after sowing. Irrigation efficiency is 90% and 80% of rain reaches the root zone. 1 mm of irrigation equals 10 m³ per hectare.
